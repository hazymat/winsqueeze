WinSqueeze 1.0: the first stable release of a Windows player for Lyrion Music Server.

- Browse your library fast: albums, artists, composers, performers, genres, folders and playlists, with search across everything.
- Bit-perfect playback through its own built-in Squeezelite, with WASAPI exclusive mode and hardware volume on DACs that support it.
- Now playing with queue, cover art and analog VU, OLED VU or spectrum analyser meters.
- Graphic equaliser inspired by classic hi-fi: 10, 7 or 3 bands, vintage or precise character, preamp with AUTO option, safety limiter with CLIP lamp, a live spectrum analyser, and a bit-perfect bypass. It docks magnetically to the main window.
- Mini player with meters and volume, always-on-top pin, back and forward navigation.
- Frosted glass skins with adjustable transparency and tint, accent colour picker (or follow the album cover), rounded frameless window.
- Updates itself in the background; one click restarts into the new version and carries on playing. A What's new card shows what changed.

Download WinSqueeze-Setup.exe (installs for you only) or WinSqueeze-Portable.exe (no install). Windows may warn that the app is unsigned until code signing is in place.
