**New**
- Radio recording: a record button beside a playing stream saves it as FLAC, with a new file for each song the station announces.
- At the end (top of Up next): choose Stop, Repeat all, or Keep playing. Keep playing adds an album or about ten tracks from your library just before the queue runs out, looking at the same artist first and then the same genre.
- Bit-perfect badge above the volume, showing the format (FLAC, MP3…) and Bit-perfect, Scaled or Not bit-perfect. Click it for an explanation, or click the format for file and playback details.
- Undo almost everything: an accidental track change, Play now (brings back the whole previous queue), Clear, removing or moving tracks, and deleting or overwriting a saved playlist.
- Track info for several tracks at once: total length, what they all share, and a per-track table for what differs.
- Long tracks (symphonies, live sets) carry on from where you left them.

**Better**
- VU meters set 0 VU automatically from each song's loudness, and the needles move more smoothly. Right-click the meters to fill the window.
- The small and large spectrum analysers now match the EQ's analyser. Click the small one to open or close the EQ.
- Meters show the EQ'd sound but ignore the volume by default (both can be changed in Settings › Meters).
- WinSqueeze's volume is independent of Windows' volume, and volume drags always end exactly where you let go.
- Lists of genres, artists, composers and performers show counts that match how the next view is grouped.
- Short windows put the meters beside the track details.
- Dismissable hints explain reordering and multi-select (Settings › General can hide them).
- Scale the whole window in Settings › Appearance. Ctrl+mouse wheel no longer zooms by accident.
- What's new lists the biggest changes first.

**Fixed**
- Search shows tracks' own titles and durations again.
- The app could hang on start after an update.
- The glass stays frosted after an update and when you click away.
