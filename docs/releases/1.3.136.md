**New**
- Undo history: Ctrl+Z keeps going back long after the Undo bar has gone, and Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z) redoes.
- Small groups share rows in grouped album grids, so artists with one or two albums no longer take a whole row each.
- The title and Sort / Group / View bar stay pinned at the top of artist, genre and composer pages.
- Recording saved: an Open in Explorer button (press E).

**Better**
- Full-window meters are an evenly scaled-up copy of the normal ones.
- Up next: a tidy single row of buttons that moves to its own line when the panel is narrow, and stays readable over light covers and see-through glass.
- Missing artwork shows WinSqueeze's own rounded placeholder everywhere.
- Settings pages scroll when the window is short.
- A touch of noise smooths out banding in the Now playing background.
- New dismissable tips for the meters, the EQ and the record button.
- Scale the whole window in Settings › Appearance. Ctrl+mouse wheel no longer zooms by accident.

**Fixed**
- What's new no longer lists release housekeeping.
- Sort, Group and At the end labels line up with their values.
- '1 track' instead of '1 tracks'.
