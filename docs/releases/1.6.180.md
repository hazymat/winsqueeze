**New**
- Multi-room: group players from the player switcher. The link button beside a player plays it together with the one you're on, and each grouped player gets its own volume slider.
- Power: turn any player on or off from the player switcher.
- Sleep timer: 15 to 90 minutes, or the end of this track, from the player switcher or the tray menu. A small moon shows while it runs.
- Shuffle: tracks or albums, from the At the end menu in Up next.
- Settings › Playback: crossfade, fades and ReplayGain for the selected player. On this PC they turn off bit-perfect, and the fidelity badge says so.

**Better**
- The player switcher menu is less see-through, so it's easier to read over the sidebar and covers.
